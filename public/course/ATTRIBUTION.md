# Course views

These six images are derived from the BYU–Idaho BIO 264 online lab, supplied as the study source for this educational aid.

- `superior-gemellus.jpg`, `obturator-internus.jpg`, `inferior-gemellus.jpg`, `quadratus-femoris.jpg`: stable frames from the corresponding animated deep-hip model views in the [leg lab](https://content.byui.edu/file/dc5a9585-91a1-40dd-b73e-0d17e0ba9594/1/leg-muscles-lab/index.html).
- `flexor-digitorum-profundus.jpg`, `flexor-pollicis-longus.jpg`: matching panels cropped from the course's paired forearm illustration, with the printed title excluded, in the [arm lab](https://content.byui.edu/file/bff57b7a-48ae-4e2d-8349-54060c98fc84/1/arm-muscles-lab/index.html).

The original URLs and labels for all course views are retained in `src/data/curriculum.json` and `scripts/source/*-lab-data.json`. The original providers retain their rights. Other course images remain hosted by the course itself. Pins, focus rings, and arrows are study overlays added by this project.
